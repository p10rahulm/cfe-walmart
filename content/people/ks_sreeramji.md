Sreeramji is a research student with the Algorithms group at the
Department of Computer Science and Automation at the Indian
Institute of Science, Bengaluru. His area of interest is applied
algorithms and his work has been around Approximate Nearest
Neighbor Search and Vector Quantization of embeddings with
geometric properties in the context of semantic search. Prior to
starting his research degree, Sreeramji worked as a Software
Engineer at Google for 3 years.

<br><br><b>Research Interests</b>
<br>
Modern embeddings exhibit pronounced geometric structure (such as the Matryoshka
property), yet existing vector quantization schemes allocate bits uniformly across dimensions. We proposed a variable bit allocation framework that partitions embeddings into contiguous buckets and allocates storage non-uniformly across them. Using a greedy allocation strategy, we improved recall by up to +8% for Product Quantization (PQ) under identical memory budgets. This work, titled Quantization Beyond Uniform Bit Allocation, was presented at the VecDB Workshop at VLDB 2026.
