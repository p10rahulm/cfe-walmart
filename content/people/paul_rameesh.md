I am a postdoctoral researcher (Project Scientist I) in the Algorithms group at CSA, IISc, since Aug 2026. I earlier did my PhD from 2021-2026 (defense pending) and M.Tech (Research) from 2019-2021 at CSA, IISc, both of these under the guidance of Prof. Anand Louis. I work in the broad area of approximation and online algorithms, with a focus on beyond worst-case analysis theme. My work is mostly based on employing continuous relaxation techniques such as linear programs, spectral methods, semidefinite programs, and convex hierarchies.

<br><br><b>Research Interests</b>
<br>
Over the last year, I worked on two projects with Pravesh Kothari, Anand Louis, and Prasad Raghavendra. The first, Refuting Independent Sets in Semirandom Hypergraphs, studies certificates for upper-bounding the independence number of random and semirandom hypergraphs. We bridged the gap between odd- and even-arity settings and established a matching lower bound with implications for sparse regimes, resolving an open question from prior work. The second, Recovering Planted Subgraphs in Smoothed Models, advances algorithms for recovering planted induced subgraphs. We extended previous results to broad families of subgraphs characterized by near-satisfiable constraint satisfaction problems and developed algorithms that continue to work under smoothed graph models.
