I am an M.Tech. Research student in the Department of Computer Science and Automation at IISc Bengaluru, working under the supervision of Prof. Shalabh Bhatnagar. I completed my B.Tech. in Mathematics and Computing at IISc Bengaluru. My research interests include stochastic optimization, stochastic approximation, and theoretical aspects of machine learning.

<br><br><b>Research Interests</b>
<br>
My research has focused on exploring the use of Kalman filtering in SGD. I have investigated a two time scale stochastic approximation approach for jointly updating the optimization iterates and estimating the covariance of stochastic gradient noise. The objective is to study the theoretical properties of the resulting method, including its convergence behavior and covariance estimation.
