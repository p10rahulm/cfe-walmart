Ritabrata Barat is a PhD student in the Department of Computer Science and Automation at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru, advised by Prof. Siddharth Barman and Prof. Anand Louis. He received his B.Tech. in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering from Jadavpur University.

<br><br><b>Research Interests</b>
<br>
His research interests lie broadly in theoretical computer science, particularly computational social choice, algorithmic game theory, and approximation algorithms. He is interested in algorithmic questions involving fairness, resource allocation, and social welfare optimization. His interests also extend to algorithmic graph theory, particularly graph and metric decompositions that preserve local structure.
