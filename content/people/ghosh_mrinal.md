Mrinalkanti Ghosh is a theoretical computer scientist working on
approximation algorithms, and hardness of approximation. He
received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Toyota
Technological Institute at Chicago in 2025, advised by Madhur
Tulsiani, and his M.Tech. from IIT Kanpur in 2012. He has also
held research appointments at the KTH Royal Institute of
Technology.

<br><br><b>Research Interests</b>
<br>
His research examines the power and limitations of efficient algorithms for discrete and continuous optimization. He develops approximation algorithms and proves hardness results, with a particular focus on linear and semidefinite programming hierarchies, including the sum-of-squares hierarchy. His work spans constraint satisfaction problems, matrix norm approximation, and polynomial optimization. A central goal is to understand when convex relaxations provide useful approximations and when they provably fail, on both worst-case and average-case instances.
