Girik is an M.Tech. (Research) student in the Department of Computer Science and Automation at the Indian Institute of Science, where he works with Prof. Gugan Thoppe and Prof. Aditya Gopalan. He received his B.Tech. (Honours) in Computer Science and Engineering with a specialization in Artificial Intelligence from IIITDM Kancheepuram. His research interests include reinforcement learning, continual learning, and generative modeling. He is broadly interested in developing learning systems that can adapt and make decisions in complex, evolving environments.

<br><br><b>Research Interests</b>
<br>
Over the past year, his research has spanned reinforcement learning and medical image analysis. A major part of his work has focused on developing scalable and efficient implementations of Reliable Policy Iteration (RPI) and studying the role of critic design in effective policy improvement under function approximation. He recently presented this work at Google Day at IISc. In addition, he has empirically studied risk-aware decision-making in reinforcement learning and worked on whole-slide histopathology image segmentation.
